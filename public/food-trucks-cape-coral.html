Food truck nights, kitchen-grade

Most food-truck spots are a window and a cooler. Class Six is the opposite: whatever truck rolls onto Del Prado gets the keys to a full restaurant kitchen and cooks real service.

▒▓░ How the rotation works ░▒▓

A different truck, the same good seat

The truck lineup rotates — that's the whole game. One night it's one crew working the line, the next it's somebody else, and the kitchen stays the same professional line it was when the space was Luigi Ristorante Italiano.

You get the truck's full menu with bar service attached: 15 drafts and frozen flights don't take nights off.

“Food trucks come in on a rotating basis and use the kitchen to work wonders.” — The News-Press, Nov. 2026
