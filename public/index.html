A new way to restaurant — bar on one side, kitchen on the other.

Class Six is an '80s and '90s retro bar and restaurant on Del Prado Boulevard. Rotating food trucks cook out of a real kitchen, 15 beers wait on draft, frozen cocktails fly in flights, mini kegs get brought to your seat, and the old-school video games are not a decoration — they're the point.

▒▓░ Two rooms, one coin slot ░▒▓

Half arcade bar. Half kitchen. Zero boredom.

Owners David Holmes and Karen Evers took over the old Luigi Ristorante space on Del Prado in October 2026 and flipped it into something the strip had never had.

◈ The bar side

Drafts, flights & quarters

15 beers on draft, house-made Zima, frozen cocktail flights, and mini kegs rolled right to your table. Between rounds: old-school video games, the way arcades meant them.

◈ The restaurant side

Trucks out of a real kitchen

A different food truck every run, cooking on the full line. No truck on the schedule? David is on the line himself — tavern pizza, sliders, nachos, and a house burger worth the trip.

“a laid-back, ‘new way to restaurant’ worth checking out” — The News-Press, Nov. 2026
