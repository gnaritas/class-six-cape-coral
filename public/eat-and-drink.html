Eat & Drink — the full rundown

Class Six runs two playfields at once: a bar pouring drafts and frozen flights, and a kitchen that hosts a different food truck every run. Here's how the whole thing works.

▒▓░ Stage 1 · The food ░▒▓

Food trucks on a real kitchen line

Truck nights. Food trucks rotate through Class Six and cook out of the full restaurant kitchen — not a window, not a reheated tray. Whatever the truck is famous for, it gets made here.

No truck? The house menu. On days without a truck, owner David is on the line serving bar food done properly:

  • Florida tavern pizza pie, the way South Florida bars do it
  • Sliders
  • Pizza nachos
  • The house burger the one the News-Press critic swore a return trip over
