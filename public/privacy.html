Privacy policy

This site collects almost nothing. Here is exactly what it does collect, and what happens to it.

What we collect

  • Messages you send. The contact form asks for a name, and optionally a phone number and email address, along with whatever you write. That information is used for one thing: getting back to you.
  • Basic server records. Like any website, the server keeps simple records of visits — pages requested, browser type — used only to understand traffic. No advertising profiles are built from them.

What we don’t do

  • No cookies are set by this site, and no tracking scripts or ad pixels run in your browser.
  • Information from the contact form is not sold, and it is not shared with advertisers. It goes to the Class Six team so they can answer you.

Questions or removal

If you’ve sent us something and would rather we hadn’t kept it, call (772) 342-0375 or send a note through the contact form and ask — we’ll take care of it.

If anything about this policy changes, the updated version appears on this page.
